The problem

Traditional dashboards launch late and age fast.

Between a business question and a useful answer sits a queue of specification, development, and maintenance. Intelligence Portal shortens that cycle with agents.

01

Every new question becomes a project

A simple change often depends on a brief, a backlog, and several rounds between business, data, and design. By delivery, the decision has already moved.

02

The dashboard stops at the chart

Even when the visualization is correct, someone still needs to interpret what changed, investigate the cause, and decide what happens next.
